Privacy

Game Hub collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no sign-in.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your scores, your badges and any game in progress are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Working offline

After the first visit, the games are saved on your device so they work without a connection. The site does not load anything from other websites while you play.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs when you load the page for the first time. We do not receive or use them.
