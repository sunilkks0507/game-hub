Rules

Goal. Get more seeds in your pile than your rival.

Your turn. Tap one of your pits that has seeds. Its seeds are sown one by one into the next pits, going round the board.

At the end of the sowing: if the next pit has seeds, pick them up and keep sowing. If it is empty, you capture the pit after it and put those seeds in your pile.

The game ends when a player has no seeds to sow. The seeds left on a row go to the player who owns that row.

Rule set. Simple classic: 7 pits each, 5 seeds in every pit, one game with no refill rounds. Other regions play slightly different versions.

Tip. Count where your last seed will land before you tap.

Settings

For grown-ups

What it teaches. Counting, looking ahead and planning. Before each move a child can count where the last seed will land.

Age. Made for ages 7 to 14. A game takes 8 to 15 minutes.

Privacy. Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Scores and badges stay on this device only.
