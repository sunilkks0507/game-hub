Rules

Goal.

Your turn.

Too little? Gilu says "a little more" and you carry on. Nothing is lost.

Rule set. Whole rupees only. The till holds up to 12 pieces. A day has 8 customers. No timer, and you cannot lose. The coins and notes are drawings, not real money.

Tip. Stuck? Tap Hint to see which piece to try and how much is still needed.

Giving change

The customer hands you a note that is more than the price. Work out the difference and tap coins and notes to give it back.

Example: a ₹7 pencil and a ₹10 note. Change is ₹3.

Settings

Grown-ups only

For grown-ups

What it practises. Making an exact amount of money, adding rupee coins and notes, and giving change. It is practice of one skill, not a test.

Levels. Coins, Notes and Change. Every level is open. There is no timer and no way to lose. Paying too much is allowed on the first two levels: the shopkeeper hands back change.

Money in the game. The coins and notes are original drawings, not real currency.

Privacy. Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Progress and badges stay on this device only.
