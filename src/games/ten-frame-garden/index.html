Rules

Goal.

Your turn. Tap an empty cell to plant a seed. Round seeds were already there. Your seeds are leaves.

Two parts. Plant the number you see. The rest fill in by themselves.

A flower grows when the frame is full. Five flowers make a garden.

Rule set. A ten-frame is 2 rows of 5. Bonds to 20 uses two frames. There is no timer and you cannot lose.

Tip. Stuck? Tap Hint to see how many more seeds you need.

Settings

Grown-ups only

For grown-ups

What it practises. Number pairs that make 10 and 20, using a ten-frame. The child sees how many are there and how many more make ten. It is practice of one skill, not a test.

Levels. Dots (no numbers, for the youngest), Fill to 10, Two parts, and Bonds to 20. Every level is open. There is no timer and no way to lose.

Privacy. Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Progress and badges stay on this device only.
