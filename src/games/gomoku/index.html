Rules

Goal. Make an unbroken line of five of your stones: across, down or diagonally.

Your turn. Put one stone on an empty point. Tap a point to preview it, then tap it again or press Place. Stones never move.

Black goes first. Stones are told apart by shape: Black is solid, White has a hollow ring.

Winning. Five in a row wins at once. Six or more also wins. If the board fills up with no five, it is a draw.

Rule set. Free-style Five in a Row: five or more wins, no forbidden moves, no timer. With Fair start on, the second player may swap sides after Black's first stone.

Tip. Four in a row with an open end is a threat. Four with both ends open cannot be stopped.

Settings

Grown-ups only

For grown-ups

What it teaches. Spotting a line of three or four before it becomes five, and blocking it in time. Older players learn to make two threats at once.

Age. Made for ages 8 and up, and enjoyed by adults. A game takes about 4 minutes on the small board.

Privacy. Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Scores and badges stay on this device only.
