Rules

Goal. Close more boxes than your rival.

Your turn. Draw one line between two dots. Tap a dotted line to draw it.

Close a box. If your line is the fourth side of a box, the box is yours and you go again. Close two boxes with one line and you get both.

No box closed? Then it is your rival's turn.

The game ends when every line is drawn. Most boxes wins.

Rule set. Classic Dots and Boxes. You may leave a box open on purpose, but you must always draw a line. 3 by 3 and 5 by 5 boards can never end in a tie.

Tip. A box with three sides is a gift: your rival can close it. Draw safe lines first.

Settings

Grown-ups only

For grown-ups

What it teaches. Planning ahead and spotting what a move will allow. Children learn to avoid drawing a third side, and later to count chains.

Age. Made for ages 7 and up, and enjoyed by adults. A game takes 4 to 15 minutes.

Privacy. Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Scores and badges stay on this device only.
