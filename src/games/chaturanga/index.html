Rules

Goal. Trap the enemy Raja so it cannot escape.

Your turn. Move one piece. Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square. Capture by moving onto an enemy piece.

You win by checkmate, by leaving the enemy with no legal move, or by capturing every piece except their Raja.

    Rule set. Shatranj style: no queen or bishop, no castling, no double step. A player with no legal move, even when not in check, loses. Draws: same position three times, or 100 half-moves with no capture or Padati move.

    Tip. Before each move, ask what your opponent can capture next.
